DIALD Framework: From Data to Decision

The Data-Informed Academic Language Development (DIALD) Framework, created by Darlin Cortico Rosario, helps ESOL/TESOL, bilingual, ELA, and content-area teachers read multilingual learners' Listening, Speaking, Reading, and Writing levels over time, spot oral–literacy patterns linked to long-term English learner (LTEL) risk, and choose targeted support from a library of lesson-plan pathways. Free for educators. No student names are stored.
